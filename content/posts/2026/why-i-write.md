I write to think. There is something about putting a half-formed idea into sentences that forces me
to notice where the idea is actually hollow.

## Writing as a filter

When a thought stays in my head, it feels complete. The moment I try to write it down, the gaps show
up. Writing is how I separate the ideas that survive contact with a keyboard from the ones that only
sounded good at 2 a.m.

## Owning the words

A blog I own is different from a post on someone else's platform. There is no engagement metric
here, no algorithmic timeline deciding who sees this. That freedom changes what I'm willing to say.

> The best reason to write on your own site is that nobody can take it away from you.

If you're considering starting a blog, my advice is simply: start. Write the first post badly, publish
it, and fix it later.
